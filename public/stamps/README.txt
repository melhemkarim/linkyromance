Put your stamp photos here (portrait 4:5, about 800x1000 px, under 200 KB, JPG):

  coffee.jpg   -> "First coffee"
  trip.jpg     -> "First trip"
  joke.jpg     -> "Our inside joke"
  today.jpg    -> "Today"

Missing photos fall back to the emoji, so you can add them one at a time.
To use different names, change the `img` paths in the STAMPS array in app/page.js.
